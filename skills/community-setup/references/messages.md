## IT team: custom domain and firewall

```
Subject: DNS record and allowlist for our new community site

Hi [IT team / name],

We're launching our community on Tightknit, and we'd like it at [community.example.com].

1. DNS: Tightknit will send us a DNS record for [community.example.com]. I'll forward it as
   soon as it arrives. Could you add it when it does?

2. Allowlist: so colleagues on the company network can open the site, please allowlist:
   - [community.example.com] (for a bare domain, both example.com and *.example.com)
   - api.tightknit.ai
   - cdn.tightknit.ai
   - assets.tightknit.ai

   A new domain has no browsing history, so web filters can block it by mistake until it's
   allowlisted.

Background: https://docs.tightknit.ai/community-site/custom-domain

Thanks!
[admin name]
```
